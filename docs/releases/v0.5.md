A follow-up to 0.4. It makes redrawing a build of shapes cheap, adds The One
Probe support, and makes a shape need the same tool its material needs.

Fixes
-----
- **Building next to shapes no longer stutters.** Redrawing a chunk that holds
  shapes read the world's light once per vertex, and re-read each shape's own
  light value for every sampled cell; repeated neighbour checks behind the
  translucent seam culling cost a full re-render each time. The light is now
  read once per shape and remembered per cell, and the neighbour checks are
  cached. A dense wall re-bakes roughly three to four times faster: a freeze
  that felt like two seconds in our test world fell below half a second.
  Roof and window shapes read their neighbours while drawing, so they are
  deliberately left uncached and look exactly as before.
- **A shape needs the tool its material needs.** Every shape used to be treated
  as needing no tool at all, so a stone or obsidian shape dropped to a bare
  hand while its material would not. Shapes now ask the material they were cut
  from, through the harvest check that knows where the block is. Wood and dirt
  shapes are unchanged; stone and metal ones want the pickaxe they should.

Additions
---------
- **The One Probe support.** Probing a shape shows its name and material with
  the mod name, the cladding when there is one, the light a glowing shape gives
  off, the arms a wall or fence has connected, and, while sneaking, the texture
  turn or mirror the paintbrush set. The tool line and its check mark come from
  The One Probe itself, so they match every other block; holds the right tool
  and the mark is green, the wrong one and it is red. Russian translations are
  included. The integration is optional: with The One Probe absent nothing is
  loaded and nothing changes.

Requirements and install
------------------------
- Minecraft 1.12.2, Forge 14.23.5.2860, Java 8. Cleanroom 0.7.x works too.
- No dependencies. The One Probe support is picked up automatically if it is
  installed.
- Drop the jar into `mods/` and start the game. Existing worlds keep their
  shapes and their orientation.
