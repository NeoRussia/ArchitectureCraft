A 0.2 patch for ArchitectureCraft: Renovated. Placement is fixed for the shapes
added in 0.1, the Double Roof Tile becomes a proper full cube, and fence
picking now matches vanilla.

Placement
---------
- **Paneling and the GregTech plaque lie flat** on a floor or ceiling click:
  cladding or pattern facing the room, the piece's top pointing away from you.
  Wall placement is unchanged.
- **The GregTech plaque faces out of every wall.** The north case was returning
  the west turn, so a plaque on a north wall came out sideways.
- **Double Siding shows its clad half.** The block turns the cladding towards
  the player on placement, using the clicked face on a wall and your facing on
  floor and ceiling.
- **Double Roof Tile is directional.** A tile placed against another copies its
  orientation, a floor or ceiling click turns it to face you, and a wall click
  keeps the click-point diagonal, so the tile no longer lands at random.

Blocks and physics
------------------
- **Double Roof Tile is a full cube** for collision and side solidity. Torches,
  levers, rails and buttons attach to all six faces, and the block no longer
  has a gap at its north edge.
- **Fence picking follows vanilla:** the selection and pick box is the post
  plus 4 px arms one block tall, while entity collision keeps the post and the
  full-width arms at 1.5 blocks. Arms are outlined when hovered, and the fence
  still cannot be jumped.
- **Mobs path around fences** instead of walking into them: shapes report their
  path node type, with fences as FENCE, solid shapes as BLOCKED, and slabs,
  stairs and roofs walkable.
- **Shapes sound like their material.** Step, break and hit sounds come from
  the base block, so a wooden fence no longer sounds like stone.

Known limitations
-----------------
- A block still cannot be placed on top of a fence shape; the click resolves
  against a side face instead of the cap.

Requirements and install
------------------------
- Minecraft 1.12.2, Forge 14.23.5.2860, Java 8. Cleanroom 0.7.x works too.
- No dependencies.
- Drop the jar into `mods/` and start the game. Existing worlds keep their
  shapes — every shape id from 0 to 116 is unchanged.
