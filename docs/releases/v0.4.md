A 0.4 patch. It fixes fence picking and chisel cladding, and adds a paintbrush
for texturing shapes that cannot be mirrored by rotating them.

Fixes
-----
- **Blocks can be placed on fences again.** Picking tested the 1.5-high
  collision boxes instead of the selection box, so a click on the cap reported
  a side face and the block landed beside the fence. Shape picking now asks for
  the selection box, as vanilla does, and collision arms are narrowed to
  vanilla's 2 px to match.
- **The chisel strips cladding from thin shapes.** Slope ends, sidings,
  paneling, plaque and wedges treated most clicks as edge clicks and toggled a
  connection that does not exist. They now always strip; walls and fences keep
  their edge zones unchanged.

Additions
---------
- **Paintbrush.** Use turns a shape's texture a quarter turn; shift-use toggles
  the mirror. Four turns, mirrored or not, is every way a square texture can
  land, which fixes converging slopes where one side reads upside down. The
  state is per block, saved with the tile, synced to clients, and a piece
  placed against a turned one inherits its orientation.
- No recipe yet, like the rest of the mod's tools: creative tab only.

Requirements and install
------------------------
- Minecraft 1.12.2, Forge 14.23.5.2860, Java 8. Cleanroom 0.7.x works too.
- No dependencies.
- Drop the jar into `mods/` and start the game. Existing worlds keep their
  shapes and their orientation.
