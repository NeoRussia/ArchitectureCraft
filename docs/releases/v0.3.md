A 0.3 patch for ArchitectureCraft: Renovated. It adds the corner wedge family
and reworks how the siding family and the two "double" shapes orient themselves
when you place them.

Fixes
-----
- **Siding answers the mouse.** On a wall, click the half of the face the bevel
  should fall towards and you get that left/right orientation, no hammer
  needed. Holding shift takes the slope's four-way rule instead, which adds the
  up/down pair, so the shift-click set is what a slope gives you.
- **Siding joins runs.** A siding placed against another shape now copies its
  orientation the way a slope does, instead of always starting fresh. Shift
  skips the copy.
- **Double Siding continues runs.** Placed against another Double Siding it
  copies that block's orientation, so a wall of them stays consistent;
  otherwise the clad half still turns towards you. Shift skips the copy.
- **Double Roof Tile** likewise skips the neighbour copy under shift, matching
  Double Siding and the ordinary shapes.

Additions
---------
- **Nine new wedges**, on a new **Slopes** page beside the nine slope tiles.
  Three families (1x2, 1x3, 1x4), each cut from one corner pyramid: the base
  band fills the block, the bands above shrink toward the apex, and the apex
  sits on the top corner of the tip block, so a set joins into one straight hip.
- **Family letters on the icons**, A, B and C, in the same faint style as the
  slope letters.
- **Glow duplicates** live on a new **Glow 2** page, directly below Glow.
- **Other** now sits below Siding, so the page list ends with the two families
  it holds.

Requirements and install
------------------------
- Minecraft 1.12.2, Forge 14.23.5.2860, Java 8. Cleanroom 0.7.x works too.
- No dependencies.
- Drop the jar into `mods/` and start the game. Existing worlds keep their
  shapes: the orientation of a placed block is stored with it, so nothing
  already built changes.

Known limitations
-----------------
- A block still cannot be placed on top of a fence shape; the click resolves
  against a side face instead of the cap.
